# Privacy Policy – gog-desktop

- gog-desktop is used only by its owner, on their own computer.
- Google user data (Google Sheets and Google Drive) is accessed only to carry out the owner's own requests.
- OAuth tokens are stored locally on that computer and are never uploaded anywhere by this app.
- No data is sold, shared, or sent to third-party servers by this app.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
